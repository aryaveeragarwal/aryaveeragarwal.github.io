I’m a 9th-grade student at CHIREC International School in Hyderabad with a strong interest in robotics, systems design, and applied engineering. I began in FTC and later moved into FRC, where I became more interested in how mechanical design, control systems, and strategy come together under real constraints.

Beyond competition robotics, I enjoy building small systems that solve specific problems. I’ve worked on projects like an Alexa-based lift mechanism and explored hydroponics to understand how automation and sustainability intersect. I’m especially drawn to projects where software and hardware interact — where code moves something physical.

I use this site to document what I build, what fails, what improves, and what I’m trying to understand next. Over time, I hope it reflects not just finished projects, but the way my thinking evolves.
